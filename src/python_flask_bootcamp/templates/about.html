About Me

Background

I am a Computer Science Engineering student based in Mumbai, blending software engineering, game development, and hands-on hardware modification. I prefer modular, practical execution over pure theory—building, iterating, and testing systems until they feel right.

Whether I am building context-aware tools, leading a team to ship educational games in Unity, or developing "FinQuest" using Python and Streamlit, my focus is on creating interactive and visually engaging digital experiences.

Hardware & Modding

Off the screen, I tinker with physical builds. I work on rebuilding retro handhelds (like configuring custom OS builds for the R36S), hardware modding with Wemos D1 Mini microcontrollers, and DIY 3D printing setups using Arduino Mega and RAMPS 1.4. I also design digital graphic assets, UI elements, and custom publication plugins.
